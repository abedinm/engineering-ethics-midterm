## The presentation

**Selected principle:** *Hold paramount the safety, health and welfare of the public* — the one
clause that appears first in all four codes on the syllabus (NSPE Fundamental Canon I.1,
IEEE Policy 7.8 §I cl.1, ACM 2018 cl. 1.2, IEB Code of Ethics Clause 1).

**Case study:** Citicorp Center, New York, 1978 — William J. LeMessurier discovers that his own
completed 59-storey tower has roughly a 1-in-16 annual chance of collapse, and must choose
between disclosure and his career.

Slides 5, 6 and 7 work through the five-step ethical decision-making cycle required by the
assignment guideline: define the moral issue, establish facts and stakeholders, map the
decision space, evaluate the options against the codes, then decide and justify.

All diagrams were drawn for this presentation rather than taken from other sources.
